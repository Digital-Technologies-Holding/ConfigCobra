# Notifications & General Settings

Configure how and when you want to be notified about assessment results, account changes, and customize your ConfigCobra experience.

## Overview

Settings for notifying you about important events in ConfigCobra. Configure Windows notifications for real-time alerts and email notifications for comprehensive updates about assessment runs, user changes, permission modifications, and other account events.

**Stay informed:** Enable notifications to ensure you never miss important assessment results or account changes. You can customize which events trigger notifications based on your preferences.

For more information, visit [configcobra.com](https://configcobra.com).

## How to Configure Settings

### Step 1: Log in to ConfigCobra

Log in at [app.configcobra.com](https://app.configcobra.com) using your credentials.

### Step 2: Navigate to Settings

Go to the **Settings** page in the main navigation menu.

### Step 3: Configure account settings

Under **Account Settings**, you can:

- Select **custom color themes** to personalize your ConfigCobra interface

### Step 4: Configure notification preferences

Under **Preferences**, you can turn on the following notification options:

#### Windows Notifications
You will get notified in your Windows notification center if an ad hoc scan finished.

#### Email Notifications
Notifies you about:
- Assessment runs (scheduled and manual)
- User changes (added user, deleted user)
- Permission changes
- And other important account events

## Notification Types

### Windows Notifications

Receive instant desktop notifications in your Windows notification center when ad hoc assessments complete. Perfect for immediate alerts without checking your email.

**You'll be notified when:**
- Ad hoc scan finishes
- Assessment completes
- Real-time updates

### Email Notifications

Get comprehensive email updates about all important events in your ConfigCobra account, including assessment results, user management, and permission changes.

**You'll be notified when:**
- Assessment runs complete (scheduled and manual)
- Users added or removed
- Permission changes
- Account events

## Account Settings & Customization

Personalize your ConfigCobra experience by selecting custom color themes that match your preferences. The theme you choose will be applied across the entire application interface.

**Theme options:** Choose from a variety of color themes to make ConfigCobra feel more personal and comfortable for your daily use.

## Best Practices

- **Enable email notifications** for comprehensive updates about all important events
- **Use Windows notifications** for immediate alerts when assessments complete
- **Customize your theme** to match your preferences and improve daily use experience
- **Review notification settings regularly** to ensure you're receiving the right level of updates

## Notification Events

### Assessment Events
- Assessment started
- Assessment completed
- Assessment failed
- Scheduled assessment results

### User Management Events
- User added to team
- User removed from team
- User role changed
- Permission changes

### System Events
- Account changes
- Configuration updates
- Important security alerts
